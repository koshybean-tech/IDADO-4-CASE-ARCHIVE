TIMELINE

Case chronology

November 13, 2022

Four University of Idaho students are murdered

Madison Mogen, Kaylee Goncalves, Xana Kernodle, and Ethan Chapin were murdered at an off-campus residence in Moscow, Idaho.

Source: Idaho State Police public investigation page

November 2022

Multi-agency investigation begins

The investigation expanded to include local, state, and federal law-enforcement partners.

Source: Idaho State Police

December 30, 2022

Bryan Kohberger is arrested

Bryan Kohberger was arrested in Pennsylvania and subsequently charged in connection with the Moscow homicides.

Source: Idaho State Police

January 2023

Criminal proceedings continue in Latah County

Public court proceedings continued in connection with the criminal case.

Source: Idaho Judicial Branch

July 23, 2025

Guilty plea and sentencing

Kohberger pleaded guilty to four counts of first-degree murder and one count of burglary and was sentenced in the case.

Source: Idaho State Police

August 18, 2025

Idaho State Police releases Moscow homicide records

Idaho State Police announced a public-records page for records related to the Moscow homicide investigation.

Source: Idaho State Police
